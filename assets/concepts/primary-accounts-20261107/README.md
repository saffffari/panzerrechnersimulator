Every package supplies `.vox`, metric sparse voxel JSON and GLB with separate factual provenance and invented staging. Athena also supplies Blender scenes. Visual material IDs are not calibrated FEA properties. These models are not yet inserted into campaign maps.

Generators and validators are in `tools/concepts/`. Geometry was authored locally; no third-party meshes, textures or historical text extracts are embedded. No remote generator was used.
