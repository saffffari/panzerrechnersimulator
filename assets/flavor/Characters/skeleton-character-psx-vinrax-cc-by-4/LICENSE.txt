Kessel's derivative preserves the source-native 27-bone skin in an authoring GLB, poses the native
skin into a relaxed/down configuration, bakes a static runtime GLB, normalizes both assets to 1.53 m,
and embeds the supplied base-color texture in one material. See SOURCE.md and bake.report.json for
the exact source/output hashes, measured geometry, and limitations of the anatomy alignment.
