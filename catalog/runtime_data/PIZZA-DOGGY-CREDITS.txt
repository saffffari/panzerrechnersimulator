KESSEL / PIZZA DOGGY ASSET CREDIT AND PACKAGING POLICY
======================================================

Selected environment, prop, structure, debris, and nature models integrated into Kessel were
created by Pizza Doggy and obtained through locally purchased asset packs.

Credit: Pizza Doggy
Vendor pages:
- https://pizzadoggy.itch.io/psx-mega-pack-2/devlog/961920/updated-the-license-agreement
- https://pizzadoggy.itch.io/psx-tech

This file is an engineering credit and packaging-policy summary, not a substitute for the retained
purchase agreement and not a grant to extract or reuse the models. Kessel packages only the reviewed
GLB closure needed by the game. It must not distribute the source archives, complete authoring
libraries, alternate formats, previews, thumbnails, or cooked models as an asset pack, SDK,
template, or standalone download.

The runtime model catalog records each integrated model's source family, asset ID, byte hash, and
`pizzadoggy-local-purchase` policy ID. The current vendor terms and retained purchase evidence must
be re-reviewed before Kessel's first public or commercial release.
