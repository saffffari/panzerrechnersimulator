"Free Tiger And Crew Kit" by AdrielCZ
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-tiger-and-crew-kit-6290669ab2cb49599f1606507626af39
Licence: CC BY 4.0

Kessel modifications: the bundled Tiger vehicle was excluded; the source-native Panzertruppe rig
was normalized and posed; five supplied face textures were assigned to persistent crew identities;
single-material embedded atlases and static station/casualty pose GLBs were baked for the current
runtime; and the included field glasses were exported as a separate physical prop.

"Enigma Machine" by Ashish0096
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/enigma-machine-30b85c037a164174a109ad6f002b9c47
Licence: CC BY 4.0

Kessel modifications: the geometry was triangulated, deterministically decimated per disconnected
part, joined, and normalized; the source textures were resized into embedded base-colour,
metallic-roughness, and normal atlases; and DirectX normal-map green channels were converted to the
glTF/OpenGL convention. The display pane is an opaque smooth dielectric in the current one-material
runtime path.

"Skeleton Character PSX" by Vinrax
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/skeleton-character-psx-ece576bbed4b4364911c7596d828a558
Licence: CC BY 4.0

Kessel modifications: the source-native 27-bone skin was preserved in a separate authoring master;
existing arm bones were posed into a relaxed/down configuration; the evaluated mesh was normalized
to 1.53 m and baked as a self-contained static runtime GLB without decimation. Its eleven-part
casualty crosswalk is a measured approximate compatibility layer, not exact anatomical registration
to the independently authored clothed crew mesh or a medical segmentation.
