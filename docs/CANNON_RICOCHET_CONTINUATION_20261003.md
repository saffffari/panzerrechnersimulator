# Cannon ricochet continuation — 2026-10-03

Real vehicle armor contact can now produce a distinct `Terminal::Ricochet` and continue the same authoritative shell. It is not an added visual bullet or a predicted impact. The actual incident velocity and contacted surface normal determine the outgoing velocity. Ordinary stopped defeats, shattered rounds, penetrations, HE and HEAT do not enter this branch. Source triangle contacts are used where the armor loader supplies them; other vehicle classes retain their existing disclosed coarse armor surfaces.

The response is an **authored, uncalibrated inelastic point-mass approximation**. AP/APCBC need at least65degrees incidence from the normal; APCR needs75degrees. Sliding tangential velocity retains0.78 for cast,0.86 for face-hardened, or0.82 for rolled/late plate; outward normal velocity retains0.20 of the incoming normal component. These are not measured historical ammunition restitution values. Projectile mass is retained, and calculated outgoing kinetic energy is strictly less than incoming kinetic energy. Lost energy is a contact-work budget, not a claim that it all became heat or a modeled rigid-body impulse on the tank. Incandescent cosmetic dust and the plate scar exclude escaped projectile energy.

The round retains its ID, origin, seed, accumulated range and total age. Gravity and transverse wind restart from its actual outgoing vector at a new force-integration epoch. The remaining fraction of the240Hz collision tick is swept normally, so deflection does not drop elapsed time. Later vehicle contacts resolve against actual retained speed; existing cover budgets already represented by speed reduction are not charged twice. The existing exponential forward-drag law, independent gravity and transverse-wind approximation remain unchanged. Cannon flight is bounded to the existing1500m forward-flight budget,30seconds total lifetime and four surviving deflections. The range budget is the existing sum of modeled forward leg distances, not a claim of exact3D arc length.

The shell is separated by2mm along the contacted normal (or four float ULPs at large coordinates). No struck vehicle or body part is exempted on the next sweep. The outgoing velocity is in the exterior hemisphere, so the exact struck plane cannot be re-entered on that immediate segment. A finite four-deflection guard bounds pathological repeated contacts. Thin adjacent objects remain eligible; this is not multi-plate penetration.

The firing vehicle exemption now ends once the cannon muzzle position is outside all applicable articulated source bounds. For legacy vehicle classes it uses their existing armor envelopes. This is a conservative launch-clearance test: concave empty portions can delay initial clearance, but returning shells have no permanent shooter/team hull exemption. Once a deflection has occurred, clearance is latched. Existing unrelated wreck/retired-vehicle collision policy is unchanged.

Impact audio and armor damage are emitted once per actual contact. The projectile-owned tracer/flight sound continues after a ricochet, and its contact receipt records actual outgoing velocity and retained kinetic energy. Stopped Bounce contacts now use compact local steel dust instead of implying an outgoing ricochet. The approved sustained fire/smoke generation is unchanged.

Validation at source freeze: standalone `rustc` using the actual ballistics module and source-extracted projectile state/helpers passed32CPU tests, including six new response/continuation tests and existing calibration/flight tests. The Game queue, live source-bound clearance, full VFX terminal routing and native presentation still require the parent's integrated build/tests and GPU review. No native performance or visual acceptance is claimed by these CPU results.
