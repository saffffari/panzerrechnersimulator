## 6. Long-term goals

From `docs/research/SIMULATION_GAPS_20260928.md` §15, ranked by how much the player feels them and how
much they matter historically:

1. **A power-limited drivetrain:** Olvar gears, RPM, L 600 C steering, and grip per surface. Today every
   tank is one speed-chase model.
2. **Survivable wounds for the lone crewman:** behind-armour debris at physical speeds, wounds, bleeding
   and treatment.
3. **A shared damage model for enemy vehicles:** components, crews bailing out, immobilisation, and
   cook-offs from what was actually hit.
4. **Complete armour geometry:** roof and belly plates, the 60 mm lower side with the road wheels as
   spaced armour, and a true 3D impact angle.
5. **88 mm gunnery:** the TZF 9b range scales, flight beyond 1.5 km, dispersion, and a loadable
   PzGr 40.
6. **Temperature as simulated state:** cold starts, frost, the crewman's own cold, and the speed and
   absorption of sound.
7. **Hull attitude and suspension:** slopes, trenches, and hull-down depression.
8. **The one crewman's movement between stations, fully embodied.**
9. **AI perception from the same physics as the player's:** hearing from the acoustics model, and sight
   from the rendered extinction.
10. **Indirect fire:** mortars and artillery with observers, the soundscape of 1942–43.
11. **Snow depth, ice and water:** river ice with load limits, fording, and bridges.
12. **Logistics and reliability:** fuel, spares, breakdowns and recovery.

## 7. Working notes for whoever continues

- **Worktree guard:**
  - Agents started in isolated worktrees are pinned to them.
  - Set them up with `git reset --hard <base>` and a reflink copy of `assets/`, `source_assets/` and
    `target/` from a prepared worktree.
  - Chain work by commits.
  - Non-isolated workflow agents work in the session's worktree, so the session must stay there while
    they run.
  - Never SendMessage a running workflow agent: it starts a second copy.
- **Judging agent progress:** use `find <worktree> -mmin -N`, processes whose cwd is in the worktree,
  and commits. Transcript timestamps are not a reliable signal.
- **Captures** go in the repository's `captures/`; films for the owner go in
  `~/panzer/shader-graphics/captures/astra/`.
- **Films and levels:** `mechanic-review` renders synced films (it refuses to overwrite, so use new
  prefixes); `tools/audio_levels.py` measures their soundtracks. The owner judges sound in synced
  video, not in isolated A/B clips.
