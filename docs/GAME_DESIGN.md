# Kessel — GAME DESIGN (Core Concept)

The pitch, the loop, the win/lose, the feel. Subordinate to `DESIGN_PILLARS.md`. This is the canonical
statement of the game; expanded facets live in the sibling design docs under `docs/design/`.

## One-line pitch
A lone tank on the wrong side of a collapsing encirclement, retracing its own trail of ruin westward
through a frozen wasteland to reach friendly lines — one long, permadeath run.

## The frame
The offensive that carried you east has failed. You are deep inside the pocket — the *Kessel* — alone
in a Tiger, the rest of your war dead or scattered. Friendly lines are to the **west**, the direction
you came from. That is the only thing you know. **Head west.**

## Structure
- **Handcrafted open-world map**, carefully designed — **NOT procedural**.
- You **start at the center**. The goal is the **western edge**, where friendly troops wait. Reach
  them and you've **won**.
- A single **multi-hour run**, **permadeath**. **FTL-style**: one tense journey of attrition, no
  respawns. Death ends the run.

## The loop
- **Slow-burn exploration + driving** through the moody 3D world is the baseline — reading terrain,
  threading ruins, scavenging, conserving, listening.
- Punctuated by **rare, super-high-stakes, super-intense** combat. When action comes it is lethal and
  decisive; then back to the quiet dread of the drive. Tension and release — weighted hard toward
  tension.

## Resources & progression
- **No food / water / survival-needs.** What you manage is **ammunition** and **components**.
- **Upgrades and ammo are scavenged along the way** — wrecks, depots, the dead. Your tank grows
  stronger or weaker by what you find and what you lose. **Attrition is the pressure.**

## Story — told by the world, not by words
- **No dialogue.** All narrative is **environmental storytelling**.
- **The spine:** heading west you **cross the ruins you yourself made coming in** — burnt-out columns,
  shattered positions, the path of your own advance, now walked in retreat. The wreckage of your
  offensive becomes the map of your escape. The wasteland is the story.

## Feel
Slow, cold, lonely, dread-soaked driving — then sudden, brutal, decisive violence — then quiet again.
Every shell counts. Every shot risks the whole run.
