# Act One environmental scene build list

These are the twelve Act One candidates from `assets/worldgen/scene_blocks/historical_scene_catalog.v1.json`, which contains 36 scenes across all three acts. This list is a production guide, not a claim that the scenes are already built or that the compact game map reproduces Stalingrad's geography.

**Start with the burned household and apartment stripped for fuel.** They share a residential kit and provide strong small-scale stories before tackling the airfield or grain-elevator landmark. Work in daylight first. Preserve clear paths for the Tiger and the player; detail should reveal how people used a place.

Dates below distinguish the event from the date of its surviving account. Reconstruct winter remains of earlier events where appropriate; do not replay September events as January occurrences. Late-surrender and post-surrender scenes need the corresponding campaign time or a separate study.

## Source and construction notes

The witness excerpt, 21 January medical report, 305th Division diary and Kulle interrogation were reopened on 13 September 2026; the 79th Division diary was also checked in this review. They establish reported conditions, not a complete ground plan. The museum diaries survive in Soviet wartime translation, the civilian account is an edited commission interview, and the interrogation is a captive's account. Preserve that provenance in scene metadata.

The existing catalog ranks the household, grain elevator, Pitomnik and Red Army House as its four Act One production priorities. The order above instead starts with reusable residential and industrial kits, and defers scenes whose location or chronology is unresolved. No catalog priorities were silently overwritten.

For each block, keep its source IDs, intended date window, district or landmark, local origin and approach path. Save props, sculpt, lights and sounds together via F7 when terrain changes are involved. Use source photographs for geometry only after identifying their subject. Exact landmarks require their correct setting; district conditions can be adapted within a compatible district. None of these require overnight/sleeping mechanics to begin building.
