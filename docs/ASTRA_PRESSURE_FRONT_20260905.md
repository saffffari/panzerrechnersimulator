# Airborne pressure front — source integration, 2026-09-05

The apparent flat front in the v7 152 mm HE clip was strongly attributable to an expanding opaque dust box intersecting the camera. The exact v7 scene-ID buffer was not preserved, so this is source/media attribution rather than absolute pixel identification. Investigation: `artifacts/astra-audit-20260905/v7-152he-apparent-pressure-front-inspection.md`.

The new `pressure_front` module replaces the authoritative HE impact-centre dust box with an explicit curved refractive surface. Debris and dust raised by nearby structures remain separate effects. The dedicated HDR pass follows bloom and precedes display grading; it never enters surface/volume temporal histories. It samples scene depth and the live TLAS to reject blocked source-to-front paths and avoid distorting foreground silhouettes. Closed protective glass is opaque to this air-front visibility test. Portable mode omits the optical cap because it has no equivalent exact scene visibility query.

The effect adds no white light or colored emission. It blends refracted existing scene radiance with the original at a maximum 0.08 weight, with displacement capped at 1.5 native pixels. Four optical fronts and 64 recent event IDs bound cost and prevent duplicate arrival dispatch. Sound or camera arrivals already scheduled survive eviction of an optical cap.

Air arrival is source-to-listener distance divided by the shared 343 m/s constant. A two-metre cabin crossing takes about 5.83 ms. The visual chooses a representative radius within the frame's actual exposure interval so a 24 fps capture can show the approaching bow even though the transit falls between frames. This is explicit presentation sampling, not calibrated optical density, a fluid solver, or slow physical propagation. The cap does not linger for the old one-second dust-box lifetime. The numerical opacity and displacement are art parameters.

Internal caps require an actual penetrating event and an interior-facing source triangle on the struck hull/turret, found by a short reverse sweep across that plate. A rejected or missing contact does not fabricate a replacement plane. Nonpenetrating 152 mm HE therefore has no internal air cap; its steel ring and structure-borne response remain immediate. Exterior HE still has an external front, depth/geometry blocked by the intact hull. General aperture diffraction and an interior-explosion/cook-off front are not yet dispatched by this narrow integration.

The impact voice uses `synth_damage_impact_with_pressure_delay`: only its existing internal-pressure source is offset by the same arrival interval before shared reflections, EQ, and limiting. There is no second pressure playback. The camera response is released on the corresponding simulation deadline; its displayed change is necessarily quantized to the next simulation/render step. External HE sound uses the same event-derived source range and arrival delay. Listener position is sampled at dispatch; moving-listener travel-time re-solving is not yet implemented.
