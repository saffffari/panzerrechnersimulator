# Kompanie im Funk (KP): Zeilen für die Stimmprobe

The voice on the company net: the company commander giving orders to the player's Tiger and to the other tanks. He is never seen. Everything he says arrives through the radio, so every line carries the `radio voice` tag, and in the game it goes through the radio chain, not the intercom. He is senior, tired, and economical with words; warmth shows only in a word or two. The crew hears him through the Funker, who answers with the lines in FUNKER.md section on the net.

## How to read this sheet

- **ID** is stable: `KP` and a number. Gaps are left on purpose for later lines.
- **Wann** is what has just happened, in plain words, so a line can be tied to a game event. Sections B and C belong to the two playtest missions.
- **Int** is intensity, 1 to 5: 1 quiet routine, 2 alert and focused, 3 urgent, 4 under fire, 5 extremis.
- **Zeile** is the text to send to the voice. Tags in square brackets are in English because the voice model reads them as direction, not as words.
- "Zwo eins eins" is the player's Tiger, the same invented call sign as in the other sheets. "Kommen" hands over, "Ende" closes, "Frage:" opens a question, "Warten" asks for time.
- Wording is mine, built from German Army radio vocabulary. It has not been checked against a period signals manual.
- `[static]` asks the voice model for a noise. It may ignore it; the radio chain can add the break instead.
