# Audition sheet: ten lines per position (9 November 2026)

For trying voices on the ElevenLabs page by hand. Choose the Eleven v3 model, paste one block,
generate, and listen for how a voice handles the climb from calm to intensity 5. The words in
square brackets are directions the v3 model acts on; it does not read them aloud. Lines come
from the five position sheets in this folder, two per intensity level.
