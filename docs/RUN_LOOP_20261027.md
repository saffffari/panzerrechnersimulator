### 4. Carry-over stakes

Canyon singleplayer has exactly one crewman (`singleplayer_crew.rs`): the other four seats are
empty posts he works, not people. "Crew permadeath" in this run is therefore the man himself, and
his death ends the run (verified above). What costs a capability inside a run is module damage
and his own wounds, both of which the existing simulation already enforces: a broken turret
traverse strains and does not turn, a dead engine does not drive (and hisses at the rear deck), a
broken track pulls the hull round, a lost sight blinds the optic, both forearms missing stop him
ramming a round (`crew_ok`). The after-action card now names every module and limb lost, so the
cost is legible once the run is over. Not changed in this pass: there is no single in-world
"told once" cue per failure beyond the physical consequence and the existing sounds; see
"still missing".

### 5. Replay variation

Every attempt draws a fresh seed (`KESSEL_CANYON_SEED` pins one). The seed already sited guns,
rifle posts and patrol phases; it now also (`canyon::variation`) moves the manifest's authored
start instant by −4, −2.5, −1, 0 or +0.75 hours on the same day (noon to just past sunset; the
ephemeris lights it) and phases the authored winter fronts. The runs above started at 11:48
(seed 29), 15:48 (seed 7) and 16:33 (seed 101) local mean time, all printed on the brief and the
card with the seed and the weather word. Unit test:
`canyon::tests::attempts_vary_the_clock_and_the_weather_phase_but_stay_in_daylight`.

### 6. Friction

Home opens the controls sheet at any moment of play (it already did; `CONTROLS.md` now says so
and the brief names it). The first 30 seconds: the brief states the direction ("Drive the ravine
north to its far end, 4.9 km away"), the threat, the keys and the rule that nothing fires until it
has seen or heard you; the Tiger starts facing along the route and M raises the field map with the
route in red pencil. Play itself stays wordless, as the owner decided on 2026-09-28.

## What is still missing

1. **A human run in the native window.** No one was at the machine during this pass and the
   launcher opens full screen, so the brief → play → death → card → Enter → new attempt flow was
   verified through the shared draw functions and the harnesses, not by driving the window. First
   thing to do with a person present: launch, die once, press Enter, finish once.
2. **A full-course completion by play.** The rule-based crew stalls at its physical reload
   (`reload_stalled`) a few minutes in, so no attempt drove the whole 4.93 km armed; the final
   fight was completed only from a 220 m staging (seed 101). The 10–15 minute target is a
   design estimate: the 2026-09-30 unarmed drive took 13.4 min and the staged last fight 2.2 min.
3. **Balance is the owner's call, not tuned here.** One 76 mm penetration is usually the end of
   the run (nine modules and the rack in both staged losses), and the first spotted T-34 can hit
   from 440 m within the first minute. The physical-truth rules forbid adjusting this by fiat; if
   it is to change, it changes through the modelled armour, spall and ammunition stowage.
4. **In-world "told once" per capability loss.** Module failures have their physical consequence
   and some sounds (ring strain, engine hiss) but no uniform single cue at the moment of loss; the
   card tells it afterwards. The living crew the audit asked for does not exist in Canyon
   singleplayer, so "loader slow / no steer" from a lost man cannot happen there.
5. **Captures of the five start clocks.** The −4 h, 0 and +0.75 h starts were exercised by the
   runs above; no still of each lighting state was taken.
6. **Controller path for the cards** is compiled, not exercised.
7. The time-of-day and weather words on the card describe the attempt's start instant and the
   storm at the end of the run; they are not a forecast of the whole run.
8. Sibling branches (`claude/canyon-wreckage-20261027`, `claude/spatial-audio-20261027`) are not
   merged here; this branch touches none of their files.
