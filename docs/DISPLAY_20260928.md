# Winter display: 1280×720 with stronger dither — 28 September 2026

The owner asked for more pixel resolution for immersion, with more dither to keep the retro grain.

## Change

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Internal resolution | 960×540 | **1280×720** |
| Scale onto the 5120×2880 display | 5.33× (pixels alternate 5 and 6 screen pixels) | **4×, exact** |
| Ordered dither (fraction of one palette step) | 0.35 | **0.55** |
| Retro strength (crushed vs smooth blend) | 0.85 | **1.0** |
| Palette, LDR dither | 31 intervals, 0.65 | unchanged |

Finer pixels hide the fixed 4×4 Bayer pattern, so its amplitude rises and the crushed image is no
longer blended 15% back toward the smooth one. Both remain display treatment only (no light is
added or removed; see `shaders/crush.wgsl`).

## Where it lives

- `tools/launch_winter_session.sh`: `KESSEL_INTERNAL_RES=1280x720`. The environment variable is the
  outer override, so it must agree with the profile.
- The courtyard's display profile lives in the ignored
  `assets/worldgen/workshops/act1-winter-courtyard-20260925/manifest_edits.json`. Apply it in any
  checkout with `python3 tools/apply_display_20260928.py [path/to/manifest_edits.json]`
  (idempotent; writes a timestamped `.before-display-*` backup; `--dry-run` shows the changes).

## Evidence

Five candidates were rendered with `winter-bench` (the fixed exterior orbit frame and the loader's
view during a cabin fire) and compared at the display's real magnification, 1:1 screen pixels.
At 1280×720, dither 0.55 with retro 1.0 carries about today's grain at finer pixels; 0.75 reads as
a coarse cross-hatch over dark areas and the sky. Comparisons (not in git):
`/home/alex/panzer/shader-graphics/captures/astra/display_20260928_*` — full 5120×2880 before/after
frames to view at 100%, and 1:1 detail crops of all five candidates.

Performance, `winter-bench dense`, 240 frames, RTX 4090: 960×540 mean 12.02 ms (p95 15.85);
1280×720 mean 12.28 ms (p95 16.17). The cabin-fire scene went from 45.7 to 47.1 ms; it is bound
by the fire simulation. Co-op renders each view at the full internal size and was not measured.

`saved_winter_profile_survives_game_boot_and_updates` passes with the new profile: the booted game
reports `internal resolution 1280x720 (one-shot runtime profile)` and the saved treatment.

## Revert

Set the launcher back to `960x540` and restore the profile from its `.before-display-*` backup (or
set width 960, height 540, ordered_dither 0.35, retro_strength 0.85).
