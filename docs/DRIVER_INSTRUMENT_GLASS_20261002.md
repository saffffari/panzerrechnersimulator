# Driver and TPZ instrument covers

The requested reference is `/tmp/Spectacle.EmwCsK/Screenshot_20261002_194401.png`: warm ivory instrument faces with dark needles and restrained glass reflections. This change adds clear covers to the existing driver fuel panel and turret-position indicator (TPZ). It does not recreate the reference's analog gauges or establish their historical identity.

`src/driver_panel.rs` constructs a closed prism from the four measured corners of source node `Hull_0240`. Its rear surface clears the current print by 0.4 mm; its nominal thickness is 1 mm. These two dimensions are project-authored, not recovered instrument specifications. The original face, readout texture, digits, print placement, and emission remain unchanged. The cover follows the hull transform and is registered as ordinary scene glass. It adds no light source, no emissive surface, and no camera-facing overlay. No painted material was changed.

The default shader backend uses the existing depth-tested glass sheen pass. It preserves opaque depth and blends a weak environment reflection. That is a bounded rendering approximation, not a refractive glass transport model: the shader uses its existing authored angular opacity curve, and the front and rear interfaces are blended independently. The cover's roughness metadata is 0.06, but the current glass sheen shader uses its shared fixed environment lobe. No new renderer exception or light-visibility bypass is introduced.

The TPZ footprint is read directly from the front-cap attributes of the named source root `turret position indicator backing` in `tiger_turret.glb`, retaining its complete authored transform. It is a 32-sided disc approximately 99.24 mm in diameter. Missing, nonplanar, or nonconvex source topology reports an error and omits this optional cover rather than inventing a replacement footprint. The reader decodes only the selected root's vertex attributes, without a second turret texture upload or mesh bake.

The existing needle protrudes about 3.98 mm beyond that face. The cover's rear plane therefore sits approximately 4.382 mm ahead of the backing: the exact support of the existing needle's complete rotational sweep plus 0.4 mm clearance. The outer face is a further 1 mm forward. The existing rounded animation axis is now shared with this clearance calculation; the needle geometry and animation are unchanged. The cover follows the turret model, separately from the hull-mounted driver cover. It introduces no new emissive material or emitter.

Total geometry cost is two additional meshes, 216 vertices and 136 triangles: 24/12 for the driver panel and 192/124 for TPZ. Each adds one glass draw per view where it passes frustum selection. The existing glass shader's environment query cost applies to covered fragments. No frame-time measurement has been made.

CPU geometry checks verified outward triangle winding and closed welded edges for both covers. The driver print has approximately 0.400 mm clearance, and its nominal thickness is preserved within 4 micrometres of the rounded source footprint. The TPZ check inspected all 360 integer-degree needle poses and found minimum clearance of approximately 0.400 mm, while the analytic support bound covers intermediate angles too. Rust regressions cover closure, winding, source footprint, complete needle rotation and unchanged print separation. They have been added but not compiled or run during the frozen hotfix performance comparison.

Before promoting this DEV change, run `driver_panel::tests` and inspect native 800×450 driver and TPZ views: digits/needle must remain readable, grazing reflection must stay restrained, adjacent hardware must occlude the covers, and each cover must track its own hull/turret frame without depth flicker. Rotate the turret through a full revolution to inspect needle clearance. Check an exterior view for proper hull occlusion. This is staged source work, not a visually accepted or shipped result.

A closer match to the reference's warm analog faces remains future asset work, not a paint or exposure change in this patch. `vehicle_materials.rs` and the active HOTFIX checkout are untouched. For transplantation, use `src/driver_panel.rs` and only the three exact game hooks in `artifacts/runtime/tpz_cover_game_hooks_20261002.patch`; do not overwrite a newer `game.rs`.
