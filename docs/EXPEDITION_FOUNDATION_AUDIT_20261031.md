# Expedition foundation audit - 2026-10-31

This is a source audit of ordinary play, not a new human or GPU playtest. Findings below are separated from already measured mission/save witnesses. Native compilation and GPU validation are coordinated by the parent agent.

## Confirmed control/physics gaps

1. **A capable last survivor can be excluded from succession.** `expedition_runtime.rs::expedition_successor_candidates` admits camp reserves and previously registered anatomical actors. Fresh rescued or disembarked people rendered by `CampPeople` have an actual finite PersonId and pose, but no anatomical actor until injury/boarding. If only such field people remain, `living_people()>0` keeps the expedition ongoing while the succession list stays empty. Waiting does not make an already capable person register. The bounded correction is explicit takeover at that person's existing source pose, preserving their health, original appearance and finite equipment. An unregistered person still seated in a carrier also needs a deliberate representation handoff; on-foot takeover alone must not be advertised as covering that case.
2. **A pushed ammunition cart uses terrain below a bridge.** `expedition_ammo.rs::update_depot_carts` sets its support from `hf.height_at`, bypassing the expedition's visible bridge deck. The existing source deck query should own support; a heightfield river bed cannot stand in for the deck.
3. **Cart movement can reposition the operator through matter.** Only cart body vertices are tested before the operator's X/Z position is directly assigned to the handle offset. A turn or blocked cart can move the operator without their swept contact solver. This happens after the ordinary camera/hand presentation, so their displayed pose can also lag the corrected body by a frame.
4. **The cart's apparent world-clearance helper is Tiger-only.** `equipment_path_clear` consults `cabin_fire` source geometry, not the environment. The cart currently lacks that world's source contact admission. The correction must test both the cart and its operator against retained source geometry and preserve both previous poses on refusal.

## Explicit feature limits

- Depot cartridges are finite and physically handed into the established ammunition system. No automatic loader collection/cart-to-rack path is present in `expedition_ammo.rs`; current operation remains one shell at a time. This is an unfinished integration, not a proven automatic-resupply feature.
- The theatre is one authored retained world with finite initial guards/counterattacks and outcome-driven site ownership. Prior fixture mission/save receipts do not establish a complete enjoyable unscripted campaign, procedural layout variation or long-run balance.
