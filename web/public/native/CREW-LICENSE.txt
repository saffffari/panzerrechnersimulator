Kessel's derivative removes the included Tiger vehicle, preserves and poses the Panzertruppe rig,
assigns the five supplied face textures to persistent crew identities, builds a unified runtime
atlas, bakes standing, seated and relaxed static station poses, and exports the included field
glasses as a separate physical object. First-person arm and binocular-hand pose frames are not part
of this derivative. See SOURCE.md and the generated bake receipt for exact modifications and hashes.
